## Questions before answers

Research notes should preserve how a question took shape, not just the final result. When reading a new method, describe the difficulty it addresses in your own words.

> This is a design-preview sample, not a claim about the site owner’s research.

## What to record

1. **Problem**: under what conditions do existing methods fail?
2. **Assumptions**: what does the method rely on?
3. **Evidence**: do the experiments support the claims?
4. **Next step**: which experiment could distinguish competing explanations?

## Turning ideas into experiments

Start with a reproducible baseline and change one factor at a time. Record the configuration, random seed, and data version.

### A reusable structure

- Research question and motivation
- Limitations of related work
- Minimal verification experiment
- Observations and uncertainty

## Keep the open questions

Unanswered questions can be the beginning of your next reading session.
