## More than a list of numbers

A vector can represent a direction or a set of features. Coordinates depend on a chosen basis.

## Linear maps

A linear map preserves addition and scalar multiplication:

$$
T(\alpha u+\beta v)=\alpha T(u)+\beta T(v)
$$

## Matrix columns

Each column represents the image of a basis vector. This connects algebraic computation to geometric intuition.
